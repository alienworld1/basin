# Payment relationships, not wallet whitelists.

Basin lets an organization approve **who** it can pay while the payee controls **where** funds are received. That split keeps a receiving-account change from becoming a new payee approval, without turning a public identity lookup into a destination directory.

## Choose a path

- [Use Basin](/use-basin) — Personal and Organization workflows for receiving, approval, payments, receipts, and revocation.
- [Build with Basin](/build-with-basin) — the supported `basin-sdk` read and preparation surface for Ethereum Sepolia.
- [Trust Basin](/trust-basin) — enforcement boundaries, evidence categories, threats, and limitations.
- [Protocol](/protocol) — the complete v1 model, from durable identity through receipt verification.

## Network and release scope

Basin v1 supports **Ethereum Sepolia** and USDC only. It does not support mainnet, multi-chain payments, public receiving-account discovery, or a generic payment API. Run the product locally with `pnpm dev`, or inspect [basin-sdk on npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/basin-sdk) and the [public source](https://github.com/alienworld1/basin).
