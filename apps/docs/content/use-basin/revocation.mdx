# Revocation

An organization can revoke its own Approved Payee relationship. Revocation stops later payment review and execution through that relationship. It does not transfer the payee's identity or receiving authority to the organization.

## What remains true

Past receipts retain their payment-time evidence. A valid receipt remains readable and independently verifiable after a later receiving rotation or relationship revocation. If review sees stale or conflicting state, it must be refreshed and reconciled instead of submitted optimistically.
