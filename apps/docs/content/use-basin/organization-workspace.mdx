# Organization workspace

An Organization workspace is the payer side of Basin. An administrator establishes the organization workspace and reviewed treasury controls, then manages organization-scoped Approved Payees and obligations.

## Separate roles, separate powers

High authority can manage payees, obligations, payment access, and treasury controls according to the shipped role policy. A routine payment operator may execute an already authorized obligation, but cannot create spending capacity, alter receiving state, approve a payee, revoke a relationship, or loosen its own controls.

Use [Approved Payees](/use-basin/approved-payees) before creating an [obligation](/use-basin/obligations-and-payments).
