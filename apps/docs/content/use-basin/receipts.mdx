# Receipts

A Basin receipt presents the payee, amount, asset, purpose, payment status, and technical evidence in that order. The technical inspector exposes the transaction and immutable payment-time fields without making infrastructure the primary story.

## Verification is three-state

`Verified` means the canonical payment event and field-level evidence match. `Invalid` means evidence conflicts. `Evidence unavailable` means Basin could not obtain enough chain evidence; it is not a failed payment and it is not verification. A successful transaction alone, or current ENS state after a later update, is not enough to verify a historical receipt.
