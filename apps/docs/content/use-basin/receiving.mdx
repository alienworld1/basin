# Receiving

Your receiving account is protected relationship data. Basin publishes a settlement commitment and generation, not a public destination lookup. The authorized payment path receives the protected settlement descriptor through a separate authorized channel and verifies it against that commitment before preparing a Router call.

## Update safely

Changing receiving creates a new settlement generation. An existing Approved Payee relationship can continue only under its shipped security and activation rules. A payer never needs to edit a destination field to follow a valid change. If the current relationship evidence is unavailable or stale, Basin asks you to check again rather than guessing.
