# Personal workspace

A Personal workspace represents the payee side of Basin. Sign in, create the workspace, choose an available Basin identity, and complete the identity claim flow. The identity is the durable public relationship anchor; it is not a wallet alias.

## What you control

You control the permitted receiving update for each relationship scope. Organizations may approve or revoke their own relationship with you, but they cannot replace your receiving account or approve themselves on your behalf. After a legitimate receiving update, your identity and an existing organization relationship remain the same while the settlement generation changes.

If an action is pending, refresh safely and use the available reconciliation action rather than submitting it again.
