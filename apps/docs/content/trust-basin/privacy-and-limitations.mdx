# Privacy and limitations

Basin keeps a receiving destination out of public identity and Approved Payee reads. Public state exposes relationship evidence and a settlement commitment/generation; authorized payment preparation verifies a protected descriptor without turning the protocol into a destination directory.

## Explicitly not claimed

Basin does **not** provide anonymity, unlinkability, transaction privacy, KYC/KYB, a global verified identity, generic fraud prevention, protection from every compromise of an authorized payee controller, a formal audit, or formal verification. It does not support mainnet, multi-chain operation, arbitrary assets, public descriptor retrieval, generic partner APIs, or automatic signing/submission.

ENSv2, Privy, RPC providers, organization governance, end-user controllers, and the consuming application remain explicit external assumptions. Read [the threat model](/trust-basin/threat-model) for failure impact and recovery boundaries.
